Who we are

Care centered on each patient

Professional home health services delivered with skill, coordination, and respect.

Supporting recovery and independence

Allied Home Care serves patients and families from its office in Alexandria, Virginia. Our multidisciplinary team coordinates nursing, therapy, personal care, and social-work services according to each patient’s needs and plan of care.

We help older adults, people living with chronic illness or disability, and patients recovering from illness, injury, or surgery receive care in a familiar setting. Our approach recognizes that home, family relationships, and daily routines can support security, dignity, and recovery.

Our mission

We provide high-quality, client-centered, and affordable home health services that promote dignified and independent living in the comfort and safety of home.

Our commitment

Our professionals communicate across disciplines, respect patient preferences, and help families understand the services and resources available to them.
