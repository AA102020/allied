Welcome to Allied Home Care

Care designed around the person, the home, and the path to recovery.

Allied Home Care is an Alexandria home care agency committed to high-quality, client-centered, and customized in-home services.

Our nurses, therapists, home health aides, and medical social workers collaborate to help homebound patients achieve the best possible health outcomes. We support older adults, people living with chronic illness or disability, and patients recovering from illness or surgery.
