Welcome to

Allied Home Care

Our mission is to provide high-quality, client-centered, and affordable home health services that support dignified, independent living in the comfort and safety of home.

Allied Home Care is a home care agency in Alexandria, Virginia, committed to providing high-quality, client-centered, and customized in-home services. Our nurses, therapists, home health aides, and medical social workers collaborate to help homebound patients achieve the best possible health outcomes.

Our skilled professionals help older adults, people living with chronic illness or disability, and patients recovering from illness or surgery receive quality care in a familiar environment. Home care can strengthen a patient’s sense of security by keeping family and friends close throughout recovery.
